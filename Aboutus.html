🏫 About Us East Africa University, Galdogob Campus

📍 Location: Galdogob, Mudug, Somalia

Established in 1999, East Africa University (EAU) is a non-profit, community-based higher education institution dedicated to providing quality education across Somalia. The Galdogob Campus is one of EAU's key branches, serving the Mudug region and neighbouring areas.​ ScholarGPS


At the Galdogob Campus, we offer a range of undergraduate programs across various faculties, including:

of Business and Management Faculty of Shariah and Islamic Studies Faculty of Computer Science Faculty of Health Sciences Faculty of Education Faculty of Economics Faculty of Social Science and Economics

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academia and industry by aligning our curriculum with the evolving demands of the labor market, producing graduates who are well-equipped to contribute to the development of Somalia.


Galdogob Campus is committed to fostering an environment of academic excellence and innovation, contributing to the growth and development of the region.
